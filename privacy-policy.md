# Privacy policy: BAROS Company PDF Export

Last updated: 14 October 2026

BAROS Company PDF Export is a Chrome extension that saves a company record from the Malta Business Registry BAROS portal (baros.mbr.mt) as one PDF on your computer. It is an unofficial tool and is not made by or connected with the Malta Business Registry.

## What the extension reads

When you click the toolbar icon on a BAROS company page, the extension reads that page in your browser so it can print it. The page can include personal data that BAROS shows, such as the names, addresses and ID numbers of directors, shareholders and company secretaries.

The extension only runs on pages under https://baros.mbr.mt/ and only when you click its icon.

## What the extension does with it

It prints the sections you chose to PDF (Overview, Share Capital, Involvements and Documents, all four by default), joins them into one file and saves it to your Downloads folder. All of this happens inside your own browser.

## What the extension does not do

* It does not send any data to the developer or to anyone else. It makes no network requests of its own.
* It does not collect any data. It keeps no page content or personal data after the PDF is saved. The only thing it stores is your two option choices (which sections, and header and footer on or off). These stay in Chrome on your computer and are never sent anywhere.
* It does not read your BAROS login, passwords or cookies.
* It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
* It does not sell or share data, use it for any other purpose, or use it to decide creditworthiness or lending.

## Permissions

* `debugger`: used only to call Chrome's print-to-PDF on the BAROS tab you clicked. It is attached during the export and detached as soon as the PDF is saved.
* `downloads`: saves the PDF to your Downloads folder.
* `scripting`: switches between the chosen sections on the BAROS page and waits for each to load before printing.
* `storage`: saves your two option choices (which sections, and header and footer on or off) in Chrome on your computer.
* `contextMenus`: adds the tick boxes for these options when you right-click the toolbar icon.
* Access to https://baros.mbr.mt/: needed to run the steps above on BAROS pages. The extension has no access to any other site.

## The PDF you save

The PDF is a file on your computer. You control who you share it with. If it holds personal data, handle it under the data protection rules that apply to you.
